# V13.27.1.36 Automatic Candidate Research

## Result

V36 adds a bounded candidate-research route over the immutable V35 source
registry. It does not add a strategy, exchange client, account read, Demo ARM,
Live ARM, approval, or order path.

## Research Contract

- Each eligible V35 candidate receives three preregistered trials.
- Candidate, trial, date range, data snapshot, cost, capital, benchmark, and
  random-seed identities are frozen before selection.
- Selection reads Development evidence only.
- Stable-neighborhood checks reject isolated parameter peaks.
- Directional, pair, portfolio, and event evidence use separate required
  metric contracts.
- Formal calculations remain in the existing Formal Validation modules.
- Duplicate or non-preregistered Formal evidence fails closed.
- Only `formal_pass` may produce `immutable_release_ready`.

## Artifacts

Each campaign writes atomic JSON artifacts and a SHA-256 manifest:

- `preregistration.json`
- `development_projection.json`
- `neighborhood_selection.json`
- `formal_route.json`
- `immutable_releases.json`
- `campaign_summary.json`
- `artifact_manifest.json`

## Bounded Smoke

The no-credential smoke over the V35 registry produced 9 candidates, 6
research-eligible candidates, 18 finite trials, and 2 data-blocked families.
It performed zero Formal runs and zero result or Locked-OOS reads. It emitted
zero Releases, approvals, ARMs, and orders.

The two blocked families remain:

- `crypto_cross_sectional_factor_v1`
- `crypto_event_driven_v1`

Their missing data is research evidence, not an engineering excuse to relax a
gate. A separate synthetic Formal fixture verifies the release boundary while
keeping `approved=false`, `demoArm=false`, and `orders=0`.

## Safety Boundary

V36 has no credential input or storage, no private API, no Trade API, no
Withdraw API, no private balance or position read, and no order submission.
Zero-winner and data-blocked outcomes remain valid and are not force-passed.
