# AlphaPilot V13.27.17 Long-Horizon Candidate Pack

## Purpose

Select five understandable research candidates each for `1h`, `4h`, and `1d`
without making matching conditions unnecessarily rigid and without forcing any
candidate to pass.

Candidate count and promotion eligibility are separate fields. A five-item
research pack can contain shadow-only or rejected designs.

## Evidence Boundary

- Target reward/risk remains at least `2R`.
- Development time, temporal validation, and deterministic symbol holdback are
  evaluated separately.
- Data from 2025-01-01 onward is locked evidence. It is reported but cannot
  select parameters or change the research tier.
- All data is local public OHLCV. No account, position, private endpoint, API
  key, Demo order, or Live order is involved.

## 1H

The previous one-bar short-rejection rules required several conditions to
coexist and became difficult to match. The five successors use 2-of-4 optional
confirmation scoring across a two-to-four-candle event window. This reduces
condition rigidity without deleting the trend, volatility, BTC-shock, or cost
boundaries.

The first direct run showed why simply loosening conditions is unsafe: all five
base definitions overtraded and had negative net expectancy. Transparent factor
attribution was therefore performed on development train, temporal validation,
and deterministic symbol holdback separately. The resulting successor pack has:

- one `research_eligible` liquidity-sweep/reclaim successor;
- one `shadow_only` BTC-aligned breakout/retest successor because fewer than
  half of holdback symbols were positive;
- three rejected definitions with negative weakest-segment expectancy.

No locked evidence selected the factors or changed the tiers.

## 4H

Failure attribution showed that combining BTC `bull` and `recovery` regimes
hid a useful distinction. The selected variants isolate BTC `bull` and use a
moderate recovery-reclaim rule:

- volume ratio minimum `0.85`
- RSI range `36-68`
- EMA200 floor multiplier `0.88`
- ATR stop variants `1.5-2.0`
- maximum holding periods `24-36` bars

All five pass the current research checks, including deterministic symbol
holdback. They are parameter variants in the same correlation group, not five
independent strategies.

## 1D

Three breakout/squeeze-breakout definitions pass the current research checks.
Two oversold-reclaim definitions have positive PF but insufficient pre-2025
selection samples and therefore remain `shadow_only`.

## Formal Workflow Verification

The seven executable event-window definitions were registered idempotently and
submitted to the unchanged formal workflow. This is a stricter check than the
candidate pre-screen.

- Both `5m` definitions failed cost stress, maximum drawdown, minimum profit
  factor, and positive average net R.
- Three of the four completed `15m` definitions failed the same four gates.
- `15m 假突破弱趋势反转 因子后继 ATR1.2` had `626` trades, profit factor
  `1.00940092`, and average net R `0.00640423`, but still failed cost stress,
  maximum drawdown, and minimum profit factor.
- The `1h` definition was checkpoint-paused during its first official public-
  data build. Its contract requires a 50-member dynamic universe with `1h`
  signal data and `15m`/`5m` execution data. Completed partitions remain
  reusable when the run resumes.
- Four bounded structural-redesign successors were generated from formal
  failures. All four remained structurally weak and stopped without promotion.

The formal result does not retroactively alter the research inventory. It does
show that the next candidate generation should target cost-adjusted expectancy,
drawdown concentration, and lower event frequency rather than merely loosening
entry thresholds. `targetR >= 2R`, temporal validation, symbol holdback, locked
evidence isolation, and cost stress remain unchanged.
