# V13.27.1.28-32 Research Renewal Closeout

## Decision

The implementation extends the existing candidate-neutral research factory
instead of creating a second S01-specific or monolithic runner. This route
keeps data, benchmark, candidate, formal-validation, OKX admission, and Release
contracts independently testable and reduces policy drift. The alternative
monolithic runner would have been shorter initially, but it would duplicate
formal-validation semantics and make immutable-boundary review harder.

- V28: causal eligibility windows, event conservation, layered readiness, and
  comparable candidate/benchmark capital paths.
- V29: `directional_event_v2`, true two-leg `pair_relative_value_v1`, and PIT
  `cross_sectional_portfolio_v1` candidate contracts with novelty checks.
- V30: bounded, resumable Program orchestration with two campaigns maximum,
  family/variant/revision/formal/backtest budgets, archived failed identities,
  and dynamic candidate artifact paths.
- Formal Core: candidate-neutral adapters, including a second synthetic fixture;
  no S01 module is imported by the core.
- V31: OKX same-exchange evidence profiles or preregistered frozen portability
  audits.
- V32: immutable Releases with `approved=false`, `demoArm=false`, and
  `orderCount=0`; exact Release-hash approval is the only human pause.
