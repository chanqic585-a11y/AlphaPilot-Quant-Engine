## Decision

Do not start paper observation, exchange Dry-run, or live trading from these
six tasks.

The useful output is negative evidence:

- These tasks are no longer vague backlog items.
- The research board can treat them as completed audits.
- Future work should design stricter, lower-frequency, or regime-aware
  candidates instead of tuning these failed candidates toward overfit results.
