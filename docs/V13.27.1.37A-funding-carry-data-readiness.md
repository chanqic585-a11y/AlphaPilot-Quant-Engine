## Causal Alignment Correction

The audit found and corrected two evidence-semantics defects before the final
report was accepted:

1. A zero-turnover candle is observed capacity evidence with value zero; it is
   not a missing turnover field.
2. Source freshness is measured from the candle `availableAt` timestamp, not
   from the candle-open timestamp. This preserves backward-only alignment and
   avoids adding the one-hour candle duration to every lag measurement.

Regression tests cover both cases. The maximum stale fraction policy remains
unchanged at 1 percent.

## Cost And Capacity Boundary

- Dual-leg 20, 40, and 60 basis-point values are stress assumptions.
- They are not claims about an account's actual fee tier.
- Historical capacity is the minimum Spot/perpetual quote-turnover proxy.
- Historical OHLCV does not prove historical order-book depth.
- Forward execution remains blocked until separate Spot and perpetual
  order-book evidence is collected under a future execution-readiness route.
