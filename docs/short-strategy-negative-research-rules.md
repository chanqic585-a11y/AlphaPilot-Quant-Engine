# Short Strategy Negative Research Rules

These rules were extracted from the V13.4.29 Short Rejection 1H failure review.

## Rules

1. Do not mistake a small set of loose conditions for a constrained strategy.
2. Do not rely only on EMA rejection plus MACD/RSI weakening for short research.
3. A loose shortScore can create overtrading even when each condition looks reasonable alone.
4. Short research must explicitly monitor rebound and squeeze risk.
5. Short research must reduce trade frequency or use stronger trigger quality before expansion.
6. Regime context cannot be ignored, but it should be evaluated before becoming a hard switch.
7. Do not rescue a deeply negative expanded backtest with small parameter tweaks.
8. Failed research strategies should be archived as benchmark/reference assets, not deleted.
9. Any future short strategy must include per-trade regime attribution before next-stage review.
10. Any future short strategy must report separated direction metrics, costs, and stop-loss sources.

## Required Future Evidence

Future short research needs at least:

- Per-trade regime attribution.
- Long/short separated metrics.
- Exit reason loss attribution.
- Slippage-adjusted metrics.
- Trade-frequency review before expansion.
- Public funding/open-interest support before renewed short research.

## Boundary

These are research constraints. They are not trading advice, not execution logic, and not live trading approval.
