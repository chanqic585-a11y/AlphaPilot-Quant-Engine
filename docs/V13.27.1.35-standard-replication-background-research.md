# AlphaPilot V13.27.1.35 Standard Replication Background Research

## Purpose

V35 implements the research track of the V33-V40 dual-track workflow. It keeps
candidate research independent from Demo and Live execution while making
bounded deterministic research runnable in the background.

Each family has at most two preregistered variants. Universal fixed 2R is not a
hard gate; exit policy belongs to each frozen candidate definition.

## Source boundary

`research/source_registry/strategy_research_source_registry.json` stores only:

- URL;
- license or usage classification;
- concise summary;
- citation;
- mechanism, formula, assumptions, and adaptation limits.

No long source passage, copyrighted implementation, or third-party branding is
copied into AlphaPilot.

## Runtime contract

The service writes:

- `state.json`: atomic queue and job projection;
- `service.lease.json`: single-writer lease with conservative stale recovery;
- `receipts.jsonl`: hash-chained cycle receipts;
- `health.json`: read-only health projection;
- `campaigns/<campaignId>/campaign_freeze.json`: content-addressed campaign
  identity and candidate inventory.

The default budget is one active campaign, one active Formal run, at most three
campaigns, six families and twelve candidates per campaign, four Formal
candidates per campaign, one structural revision per family, and the inherited
96 full-backtest budget.

The V35 executor currently freezes nine candidates and reports two data-blocked
families. It intentionally stops at `ready_for_prefilter`; executable replay for
the registered families is the next research stage.

## Dual-track isolation

The V35 receipt is appended to the V33 master and research ledgers. The Demo
product ledger remains unchanged. The cross-track ledger is touched only if an
immutable Release becomes ready. No exact approval, Demo import, Runtime ARM,
private account read, Trade API, Withdraw API, or order side effect is allowed.

## Operator commands

```powershell
# One deterministic cycle
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\run_strategy_research_cycle.ps1

# Bounded 24-hour background window at five-minute checks
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\start_strategy_research_background.ps1 -MaxCycles 288 -IntervalSeconds 300

# Pause/resume/status
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\pause_strategy_research.ps1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\resume_strategy_research.ps1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\get_strategy_research_status.ps1
```

The background launcher owns no exchange credential. It exits after the bounded
cycle count or operator stop and never installs a hidden optimizer.
